Found it and it's fixed: the checkout 500s came from a sandbox API endpoint left in the tax-svc v2.14.0 config. I rolled tax-svc back to v2.13.2 at 15:12 UTC (per the runbook — rollbacks are pre-approved for sev2), and the 500 rate has been at baseline 0.02% since 15:19, stable through 30 minutes of monitoring.

The chain of causation: v2.14.0, deployed at 13:47 UTC today, added a ZIP+4 enrichment call to Avalara but shipped with `AVATAX_HOST=sandbox-rest.avatax.com`. The sandbox endpoint rate-limits at 10 requests/second and peak prod traffic is around 45/s, so tax-svc started getting 429s and retrying — three retries at 2s backoff, up to 6 seconds of added latency, which pushed tax-svc p99 to 8.4s against a 210ms baseline. payment-orchestrator's downstream timeout is 5s (`config/timeouts.yaml` line 22), so those calls timed out and raised TaxLookupTimeout, which checkout-svc catches as a generic exception and rethrows as a 500. It only looked intermittent because off-peak traffic falls below 10 req/s and no 429s fire. The window I traced was 14:02–14:40 UTC: 137 500s, all on POST /v2/checkout/complete.

Two things I didn't expect, both worth your attention. First, checkout-svc collapses a typed TaxLookupTimeout into an untyped 500, which is why this needed request tracing instead of showing up as a legible error. Second, the deploy checklist has no config-diff review step for vendor endpoints, so a sandbox host reached production unreviewed — I filed TAX-2212 proposing one.
